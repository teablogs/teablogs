Hello, readers! 👋 Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

**“You do not need to leave the room. Remain sitting at your table and listen.”**
— Franz Kafka

In a world that constantly tells us to move faster, sometimes the most important journey begins by simply stopping and observing.

I believe life is a continuous process of learning — learning about the world, technology, people, and most importantly, ourselves.

Technology fascinates me because it is not just about computers and code; it is about ideas, creativity, problem-solving, and the endless possibilities of what we can build.

At the same time, my interest in psychology reminds me that behind every technology is a human being — someone with thoughts, emotions, habits, dreams, and fears.

I’m still figuring out my own path, but I want to build a meaningful career in technology while continuously growing in knowledge, character, and understanding.

My faith in God gives me direction when the path isn't always clear, and reminds me that success is not only about where we reach, but also about who we become along the way.

Perhaps life is less about finding one perfect destination and more about staying curious enough to keep exploring.

So, this blog is a small part of that journey — a place where I can share what I learn, what I discover, and sometimes, simply what I think.
